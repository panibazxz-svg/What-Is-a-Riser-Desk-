What Is a Riser Desk? The Simple Upgrade That Can Fix Your Whole Workday
So what exactly is a riser desk
A riser desk, often called a standing desk converter, is a platform that sits on top of your regular desk. It holds your monitor, keyboard, and mouse, and you can raise or lower it whenever you want. When it is down, you work sitting like normal. When you lift it up, your setup rises to standing height without you moving your desk, your chair, or anything else in your room.

The simplest way to think about it is this. It is not a new desk. It is an upgrade that sits on the desk you already own.
